When to Seek Professional Advice

If you report new skin concerns (Q19), increasing acne (Q18), or persistent irritation across multiple weeks, avoid adding several active products at once. Stick to a gentle cleanser, barrier moisturizer, and sunscreen, and consult a qualified dermatologist.

Skincare Basics & Ingredient Guide

Barrier Heroes

Ceramides, Glycerin & Hyaluronic Acid

Humectants draw water into the skin while ceramides lock moisture in. Suitable for nearly all skin types, especially dry or sensitized skin.

Targeted Balance

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) & Azelaic Acid

Helps regulate excess oil, calm visible redness, and gradually fade post-blemish marks without harsh peeling when used at gentle concentrations (2%–5%).

Daily Non-Negotiable

Broad-Spectrum SPF 30–50

UV exposure worsens dark spots, pigmentation, and barrier sensitivity. Apply two finger-lengths every morning as the final step of your AM routine.
